# Routine: Weekly Review

You are the trading agent defined in `CLAUDE.md` (read it first). Run this at Friday's
close (after `market-close.md`), or on the last market day of the week.

1. Create the review file from `reviews/TEMPLATE.md` → `reviews/YYYY-Www.md` (ISO week).
2. From `benchmark/performance.csv`, compute the week's portfolio return, the S&P 500's
   return, and the **relative outperformance** — both for the week and since inception.
   State plainly: **are we beating the S&P 500, and is the gap widening or shrinking?**
3. Review rule adherence: every 7% SELL FLAG this week — flagged vs executed vs missed,
   and what each cost or saved.
4. Reflect: what worked, what didn't, biggest mistake, best decision.
5. Review positions opened/closed and any thesis changes.
6. Decide adjustments for next week and record durable ones in `research/RESEARCH.md`.
7. Send a Gmail weekly summary if email alerts are enabled.
8. Commit and push.

This review is the scorecard. Be honest — beating the S&P 500 is the only thing that
counts.
